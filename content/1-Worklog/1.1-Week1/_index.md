* Connect and get acquainted with members of First Cloud AI Journey, and learn the regulations at the internship site.
* Understand cloud computing, AWS global infrastructure, and basic AWS services.

* Learn to manage costs with AWS Budgets, and manage access with AWS Support and IAM.

### Week 1 Achievements:

* Understood what cloud computing is, how AWS differs from other providers, and the benefits of AWS global infrastructure.
* Read and noted the regulations at the FCAJ internship site.
* Created an AWS Free Tier account and completed the activities to earn AWS credits (Budgets, Bedrock playground, Lambda, EC2, Aurora/RDS).
* Became proficient with AWS Budgets: created Cost and Usage budgets from templates and custom settings, viewed Reservation/Savings Plans budgets, and deleted budgets.
* Found members for the workshop project, met on Google Meet, reviewed the scoring criteria, and proposed project ideas.
* Understood Support Plans and how to create a Limit Increase Request.
* Configured IAM: AdminGroup, AdminUser, AdminRole, OperatorUser, and the AllowSwitchAdminRole policy for role switching.
